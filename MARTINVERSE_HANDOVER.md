# PART A — THE WAR, MONTH BY MONTH

The dramatic spine: a frozen line becomes a trap → the unthinkable weapon is used and the doctrine
dies → near-defeat → the turn → liberation at terrible cost → a second crisis → the long tail.
**Plan XXI clock:** J+8 covering force · J+21 full mobilization · J+28 of combat supply.

## 1984
**OCT — the detonation.** Russia nukes **Brześć (22 Oct, J+0)**; France answers with **six counter-force strikes** (the Kronstadt & Sebastopol fleets — no cities); the Assemblée votes war 506–9; **Mathieu's "the card stays face-down" doctrine dies here, as tragedy.** The Russian **GROM** offensive (14 armies, ~5,000 tanks) opens; the covering force meets it J+8 *in a retreat*. **J+2:** a D.900 downs a nuclear bomber near Piotrków (an unarmed fission device aboard — the regime has more bombs, delivered by aircraft, and aircraft can be shot down). **Family:** **Paul** is under the Baltic on J+0; **Élise's** tank loads at Mourmelon on J+1 and detrains at Łódź on J+8, driving toward a front coming at her.

**NOV — the Battle of Warsaw / "Miracle at the Vistula" (30 Oct–5 Nov).** The original Miracle (no 1920 echo). Radzymin/Ossów/Wieprz; **Élise's 501e reaches Ossów 31 Oct, first shots 15:47 at 1,800 m**; Radzymin changes hands three times; **3 Nov the Franco-Polish counterattack crosses the Wieprz behind blue flares** ("Niebieskie flary! Francuzi!") and the Guards armies are destroyed (400+ tanks). Honour **OSSÓW 1984 / PREMIERS AU COMBAT** (held by AA, not air supremacy). GROM then crests (Königsberg 15 km, Kielce, Lwów falls, Rivne destroyed) and dies on its own logistics — Plan XXI was a perfect *mobilization* plan, not a *battle* plan: "the army arrived on time and found the war in the wrong place." **Family:** Penthièvre, Daniel, Georges, Marc, then Simon/Louis Henri/Andy reach the front; **Andy's** night river-probe at Wehlau ("7 Russian dead on the western bank, none of his own").

**DEC–MAR '85 — "L'Hiver des machines."** The front freezes; France out-produces Russia (~340 D.900s, 220 G4s, 4.2M shells by March). Joseph's first nuclear deferral: **"Pas ce soir."**

## 1985
**APR — Königsberg & the turn.** Russia nukes **Königsberg (17 Apr, 06:14)** through a 40-second radar gap; Lucie ends restraint. **19 Apr: Operation AURORE opens (05:00 barrage) + the four-city retaliation** (Kaluga, Savinkovgrad, Novgorod, Rostov; 2-for-1; "two for Brześć, two for Königsberg"). **Family:** AURORE H-hour — **Élise** crosses at Ossów 06:32, **Penthièvre** south of Radom 06:45 (over **Georges's** overnight bridge), **Louis Henri "pulls the lanyard."** **Andy walks into the Königsberg footprint for six hours** carrying out survivors → the radiation that earns his VC.

**MAY — the great encirclements.** The **Kielce Pocket** (62,000 surrender, 3 May) → the **Battle of Małopolska / Op FOUDRE** (Joseph's "20th-century Cannae," **194,000 surrender, 14 May** — **Penthièvre takes Jarosław**, cutting the corridor). **Andy** evacuated to Hanover as the radiation arrives.

**JUN — the Tilsit Line / Lwów.** Athens votes to push to the pre-1941 borders; **Lwów liberated (13 June, a Polish city).** The **births begin** (André, end-June).

**JUL–AUG — Operation MARAIS** (the KRTL Bagration, *through* the Pripyat marshes Joseph said were impassable). **Georges & Daniel build the corduroy roads and 12 bridges**; Babruysk (20 Aug), Mazyr (25 Aug). **Élise fights at Kremenets, then enters the liberated UNR capital Rivne.** More births across the summer.

**SEP–OCT — Minsk & the Baltics.** **Minsk falls 5 Oct** after brutal urban fighting (**Marc** gives last rites eleven times); **Georges's Bug crossing costs his company 43 dead**; Vilnius falls 19 Oct. **Andy is medically released, comes home, and meets his son André; Daniel and Georges both learn they're fathers when Minsk falls and the mail catches up.** ~22,000 French dead.

**NOV — Operation DAVOUT** (the "no winter pause" — the decision that pulled the war's end forward). **Uman (18–22 Nov): Élise breaks the southern line, Penthièvre kills 6 tanks for 2.** **Joseph's 5th star gazetted 24 Nov.** Orsha assault opens 26 Nov — 91 dead in a day.

**DEC — the winter advance (day-by-day).** **Orsha falls 3 Dec** (two sappers drown laying a pontoon at −18°); **Riga falls 22 Dec, the Latvian flag over the castle on Christmas Eve** (second nation freed); Mahileu 28 Dec. **Penthièvre loses 4 tanks at Smila.** **Marc says Christmas Mass in a barn, in Latin.** ~25,000 dead.

## 1986
**JAN — the deep cold & the first mutiny.** Pärnu (6 Jan); the thirty-minute frostbite rule; **Élise's tanks crack in the cold until SNCA heating blankets arrive (9 Jan).** **Krasny surrenders 22 Jan — the NCOs march the officers out under guard** ("we have resolved the question of command"): the first witnessed mutiny. **−31° on the 29th** (three men die when a stove fails). Casualties recalibrated to **~50,000.** **Joseph's red-notebook letter to Élise and his arctic-stove requisition (deadline 15 Feb).** **The blue-lips rescue of Mathieu & Gabriela to the Élysée (12 Jan).**

**FEB — the border hold & SIMOUN.** The council decides **not to cross into Russia proper** (20 Feb; the war aim is liberation, not invasion). The **SIMOUN** fuel additive reaches the depots; the stoves arrive (15 Feb — Joseph's deadline met). **Montpensier reaches the front (12 Feb).**

**MAR — Operation VARANGIENNE (approach).** A double envelopment of Kyiv (Russian since 1943). **Georges builds the Pripyat causeways** that make the northern pincer possible; **Simon's 3e RI crosses the marsh first; Élise halts hull-down at Vasylkiv. Élise's concussion** (a mortar 4 m from the turret) → **the aid-station reunion with Marc** (the sunglasses "meant for the dying"). The ring is left open to the east — Joseph wants the garrison to choose.

**APR — the Battle of Kyiv (23 days).** Restraint geometry: the eastern gap is a kill-zone (everything entering bombed, everything leaving let go); the western city is spared. **2 Apr Khotiv (Élise's track mined); 7 Apr Simon's first kill (the Russian in the bathtub); 14 Apr Holosiivskyi — Élise loses 3 tanks and watches a crew's driver burn from 40 m and does not stop; 18–20 Apr the Lavra — Marc walks 409 people out under a white flag, the column is fired on at 200 m, he takes a dead man's Nagant and holds it 40 minutes shielding a child and never fires (the tremor that never stops); 21–23 Apr the Paton Bridge — Simon's 3e RI, 47 of 120 across in 9 minutes, over Georges's 18-hour pontoon. Kyiv falls 23 Apr, 17:00.** ~12,800 French dead. **Marc's Assemblée testimony (23 Apr, the trembling glass) and the roofless "Martin Mass" (25 Apr — "On est sept. Il en manque cinq").**

**MAY — Tallinn & the south restaged.** **Tallinn falls 22 May** (**Georges blows the third-line breach 16 May, Chartres pours through; Andy takes the surrender on Pikk Street — now Major + VC; Daniel clears the night mine-lane**). Ier Corps moves south for the 3 June Inhulets crossing.

**JUN — the twin dawn & the Russian Summer Offensive.** **3 June: MISTRAL** (south — **Élise across the Inhulets by midday**) **and the Russian offensive at Vitebsk (04:50)** open the same morning. **Penthièvre's regiment pivots 90°** to meet it; the **Lepiel pocket (9–12 June)** destroys the leading Russian echelon — but a second echelon comes, and the weight shifts to **Mahilyow**, where the IIIe is caught mid-move and the pocket forms.

**JUL — the Mahilyow pocket & the long retreat.** The relief falls **3 km short**; only the rearguard is trapped; **~one-third breaks out.** **When his CO Aubertin is killed, Penthièvre takes the regiment, then the pocket; his tank is knocked out 40 m short of the Drut (Tanguy & Le Goff killed, Mahé burned) and he is taken in by Belarusian partisans → "Pan Karol." Georges is captured on his own bridge at Choklovo.** **Two "disparu" telegrams reach Paris.** The Belarusian front withdraws ~200 km — the first retreat since '84; **Britain commits a fresh army by sea.**

**AUG — Second Minsk, the Caucasus, Ukraine sealed.** The line bends to the city's edge; **on 10 Aug a fresh German army strikes the salient's flank and relieves Minsk** — the Coq-and-Aigle moment (a German Leopard and a French G4 firing east 40 m apart). The last Russian offensive is destroyed. **The Caucasus front reopens (12 Aug)** — Georgia invaded. MISTRAL resumes (15 Aug); Kherson falls; the Dnieper's right bank cleared.

**SEP — the Race for the Dnieper.** Hundreds of **barges** throw three corps across before the October mud (Sumy, Kharkiv's approaches, Odesa half-encircled). **The war decree (15 Sep)** — replenish the IIIe, raise a new VIe Corps, the *baccalauréat de guerre*, conscript 17-year-olds — and **Joseph, now made CEMAT, signs in his own nephew François; Sophie cannot look at him.** **Constance's 25-word ICRC message** goes east to the captured Georges, telling him he's a father.

**OCT–NOV — winter prep & the Straits.** Joseph will fight through winter; **Étienne's "winter of begging"** raises a coalition Caucasus rescue capital by capital; **the Marine forces the Turkish Straits (Nov)** — Dardanelles, Istanbul shelled, the Black Sea and the Odesa supply line opened.

**WINTER '86–'87 — Operation OURAGAN.** The whole front struck in one hour so a broken army has nowhere to send its last reserve; **GLAIVE puts French boots on Russian soil for the first time since 1812.**

## 1987 — the long tail (un-fixed / unwritten)
The Russian army is broken **as an offensive force** at Second Minsk but stays broken-but-defending; the war **grinds into late 1987** (Europe the victory, the Near East — Caucasus, Turkey, the Straits — the long tail; peace in late autumn 1987). **The collapse and endgame are unwritten — start there.**

# PART B — THE FAMILY ARCS

The conceit: **one family runs and bleeds for the Republic at once** — the desk, the map, the turret, the dispatch, the money. "Twelve uniforms. Four countries. Three seas. One family." Their sacrifice is also the President's shield against the charge that she is "removed from the tragedy."

## The ones who decide

**Lucie** — President; former Finance Minister; **deeply Catholic** (Gabriela put the rosary in her hands "before they could hold a pen"); height 156 cm; her tell is "Mathieu's stammer redirected into silence." **Arc:** the six counter-force strikes → the agony of restraint (four deferrals, **"pas ce soir"**) → Königsberg breaks her → the **Salon Murat: she prays the Hail Mary in Gabriela's accent, then signs the four-city order MARIE-LUCIE AUGUSTINE MARTIN-LAURENT, "Marie first"** → governs *while* fighting → **recuses herself from Joseph's CEMAT appointment** to dodge the nepotism charge ("the generals choose the general who saved them; the President's sister does not choose her brother"). Survives the war. Signature pain: "my war took her husband, left a child." Calls Nounette every morning; her instruction to Joseph, never obeyed: **"Repose-toi."**

**Joseph** — the **desk-strategist who never fires a shot**; "the boy from Algiers who went to war at 18"; bad leg, the Croix de Guerre, Cheshire "in the bones." Major-Général → **5th star (Nov '85) → CEMAT (autumn '86)**. Author of Plan XXI, AURORE, FOUDRE, MARAIS, DAVOUT, VARANGIENNE, OURAGAN — placed beside Moltke/Grant/Kléber. **Arc & interior life:** keeps a **red-leather notebook** of every dead soldier (name, rank, age, town, date — Esther's 1982 gift); reads the reports each morning for his family's names ("**le rien est le meilleur mot de la langue française**"); carries the troops' names for him — **Le Boucher de Brienne, Martin-sans-gants, Le Frère** — and "continues." The tragedy: **his strategy consumes his own blood** (Penthièvre captured, Georges taken in the withdrawal that was his plan; then he must **sign his own nephew François into the army**), "and he says nothing, because there is nothing a CEMAT is permitted to say." His formula: **"La décision descend. Les noms remontent. Je vis dans les deux sens."** To Élise: "You did it like your mother. Your mother never lost anyone she had decided to keep."

**Étienne** — Foreign Minister; Parisian *titi*, 192 cm; reads "the space between what people say and mean"; the family's **"dog"** — the protector who **"never shakes."** **Arc:** delivers the declaration of war → the diplomatic counsel at the Salon Murat → the **Caucasus "winter of begging"** (door to door, "spent his pride a piece at a time") → the defuser of family crises (the proxy-marriage fight; Marc's procedural shield). The keeper of promises — to **Mme Renaud**: *"S'il arrive quelque chose à Georges… c'est moi qui viendrai vous le dire. Pas une lettre. Pas un gendarme. Moi. En personne. Dans cette cuisine."* Writes his son Marc letters "like AFP wire dispatches, with subheadings."

**Mathieu & Gabriela (the dying founders).** Mathieu (b. March 1895; the 1918 German leg-wound) watches the adversary of his life fall and his doctrine die before him — still sharp, lecturing infants on the mining code and Article 32 ("the only man in France who could put a baby to sleep with a lecture on Article 32"). Gabriela (Italian, Catholic, "reads bodies") fades (the cyanosis); reverts to Italian for finality — **"Vai, Mathieu."** **Their infant son Louis Henri lived two days (Vichy 1944) — the name on the locket at her throat**, now alive again as "Loulou." The babies "save them"; Esther's withheld dread: "a house where the very old and the very new are laid in the same bed is a house preparing, gently, for something." **Locked to die 1986 — but reopened when the ending was un-fixed (see Part C).**

## The ones at the sharp end

**Élise** (the soldier-protagonist) — 501e RCC tank commander; Sous-lt → **Capitaine** (Kyiv); **Croix de Guerre ×3, Chevalier LdH, Médaille des Blessés.** Ossów (first shots) → AURORE → Kremenets → Rivne → Uman (breaks the line) → the cold-cracked tanks → Kyiv (track mined, watches a crew burn, the concussion) → MISTRAL across the Inhulets → the Dnieper barges → OURAGAN. The only child who follows Joseph's trade and keeps his kind of notebook. **Home thread:** writes lustful letters to **Rémi Caron** (petroleum engineer at the Grandpuits refinery) on a tank maintenance form at −15° — *"Je te gèle les nichons ici…"* — and declares she wants a child ("**c'est le seul ordre que j'ai jamais donné qui me fait trembler en l'écrivant. Embrasse Maman. Ne lui montre surtout pas cette lettre.**").

**Penthièvre / "Chafi" / "Pan Karol"** (the prince in the line) — 1er Cuirassiers; Sous-lt → **Capitaine.** Saint-Cyrien from day one. Jarosław → Lwów → Uman → pivots 90° at Vitebsk → **at Mahilyow his CO Aubertin is killed and he takes the regiment, then the pocket** (*"…ici le capitaine d'Orléans. Le chef de corps est mort. Je prends le régiment"*) → his tank knocked out at the Drut (Tanguy & Le Goff killed, Mahé burned) → **taken in by Belarusian partisans, recognized by his uniform — "Frantsuz… the Frenchman."** His **Saint-Cyr "soldier's Russian"** gives the cell "ears" (he reads radio nets, interrogates prisoners); **"the Professor"** (a Minsk historian in the marsh) recognizes the Bourbon name — *"Vous êtes de cette maison?" — "J'en suis. Mais ici je ne suis qu'un capitaine"* — and keeps the secret. **Deliberately not a hero** — "one fighter in one cell among thousands… a grain in a landslide… he survived the winter; that was the whole of it." **Reported "disparu." His return is unwritten.**

**Georges Diop** (the engineer) — half-Senegalese orphan (father **Amadou Diop** died on a steel casting floor; raised by his grandmother **Mme Renaud**); polytechnicien; 5e RG; Sous-lt → **Lt.** Étienne's surrogate son. Builds the river crossings (the Radomka, the Bug — holding a drowning sapper — the Pripyat causeways, the Bucha bridge, the 18-hour Dnieper pontoon, Podil's water, the Tallinn breach) → **captured on his own bridge at Choklovo** ("the work of his hands the reason any lived, and the thing that fixed him to be captured"). **In captivity he is treated as an exhibit, not a prisoner** — guards bring friends to the wire, a man measures his skull **with calipers**; he refuses to perform, holds his face level and **runs load tables behind his eyes.** The double-bind: **"To France he was too African to name his child [the naming law barred *Amadou* → *Amédée*]. To Russia he was nothing but African."** **He does not know he is a father** until **Constance's 25-word ICRC message** (via the Swedish Embassy): *"Georges — tu es père. Un fils, Pierre Amédée… Tiens bon. Constance."* **Repatriation and the father–son reveal are unwritten.**

**Marc** (the chaplain) — Jesuit, speaks Russian; aumônier (Ier → Ve Corps); **Croix de Guerre palme** (kept in a closed drawer). Last rites across the front → the barn Christmas Mass → **the Lavra** (evacuates 409, survives the massacre, takes a Nagant but never fires — the permanent **hand tremor**) → **the Assemblée testimony** (won't touch the glass of water so the country won't see his hands shake; at the end he lifts it and the water trembles on camera) → the roofless **"Martin Mass"** (Latin, "because Latin doesn't take sides"; the wine spills; **"On est sept. Il en manque cinq"**). His Lavra letter to Étienne: *"J'ai pris une arme… Je ne l'ai pas utilisé… Mes mains tremblaient. Elles tremblent encore… Prie pour moi. Pas pour ma sécurité. Pour mes mains."*

**Andy Carington** (the in-law in British uniform) — Coldstream Guards; son of the Foreign Secretary; Lazard banker; naturalized French; the **British Warm** coat; speaks "phonetically English" French. **Captain → Major; Victoria Cross** (Königsberg). Wehlau → **the Königsberg walk-in (six hours in the footprint) → mild radiation (recovered, no cancer, lifelong surveillance the family doesn't know about)** → comes home Oct '85 and **meets his son André** (Nounette tells him in person; Lucie wordlessly puts the baby in his arms) → recalled for the Baltic → leads through all three Tallinn lines and **takes the Russian surrender on Pikk Street** → holds the seaward hinge at Second Minsk → OURAGAN.

**The briefer soldiers:** **Simon** (3e RI; Soldat → Caporal-chef; Médaille Militaire — his first kill in the bathtub, then the Paton Bridge, "won't speak of the bridge for eleven years"); **Paul** (SNA Casabianca; EV2 → EV1 — the silent Arctic war hunting Russian SSBNs, no decoration); **Louis Henri** (40e RA; Caporal → Sergent; the *député* who voted *oui* then volunteered — "pulls the lanyard" at AURORE, then fires on Pechersk, "killing the city he voted to liberate, one fire mission at a time"); **Daniel** (13e RG; Caporal → Sergent — the engineer who learns he's a father when Minsk falls, clears the Tallinn mine-lane); **Chartres** (152e RI; Soldat 1C → Caporal — Daugavpils, the Tallinn breakthrough); **Montpensier** (110e/3e RI; the youngest in the line — the reunion with cousin Simon before the marshes, the Paton Bridge on the second wave); **François (Joinville)** — conscripted by the '86 decree, signed in by Joseph, **posting deliberately left open.**
