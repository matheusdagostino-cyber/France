# I. METHOD & TONE (the editorial constitution)

- **No OTL imports, ever** — not even oblique. Hiroshima, MAD, Pearl Harbor, "holocaust," the 1920 Miracle at the Vistula were all hunted out and stripped. This world imports *nothing* from ours. Real historical *people* who appear (the Belarusian martyrs, the Rada presidents) are **reattributed** to this timeline's regime ("same graves, different killers"); invented characters are "the Fourth Republic's own," never repurposed.
- **No Soviet Union; nothing "Soviet."** The occupier is Savinkov's Ideocratic Russia. Architecture is "brutalist," not "Soviet." **Always pre-occupation place-names** (Petrograd not Leningrad; Proskuriv not Khmelnytskyi; Stanisławów/Tarnopol; Reval; "Vilna" only on Russian maps).
- **Russia is deeply Orthodox and pious**, NOT atheist — gilded monasteries, the Lavra as the regime's spiritual jewel; political officers (SZRS) are *believers*.
- **Atomic tech ~a decade behind OTL**: a few small, ruinously expensive, aircraft-delivered fission bombs; no missiles, no MAD, no megatons. The order rests on secret mutual ambiguity.
- **The loi des prénoms (germinal an XI / 1803)** is in force: legal given names must be French / biblical / saints' forms (surnames untouched). This is a recurring engine of cruelty (Amadou → Amédée).
- **Register:** spare, somatic, present-tense interiority; **bilingual** (French line + English gloss for dialogue and letters); refrains "told at the table for forty years." The author's repeated direction was **"just write"** (drop "this person thinks in…" framing) and **be maximalist/granular** ("the lizard brain craves it… down to the sub-unit of the subunit" — this produced full Excel ORBATs).
- **Method:** canon is built, revised, and **retconned in place**; the assistant flags "my construction vs. locked canon," offers retcons, and the user **un-fixes** settled events at will (e.g., the endgame). Treat §XII as the source of truth on conflicts.

# II. THE WORLD

- By 1984 the world is **two armed blocs plus poles** (NOT three blocs): the French-led **Entente / Athens Pact** vs. the Russian-led **Eurasian Bloc** (Russia + Turkey); a fragmented Arab world; non-aligned **Italy**; neutral **Sweden**; **China** a watching fourth pole.
- Both France (**1956**) and Russia (**mid-1960s**) are **secretly nuclear**; satellites from **1965**; everything ~a decade behind OTL.

# V. THE CAPTIVE NATIONS & EXILE PARIS (the deep tapestry)

The richest seam. Four exile councils sit in Paris; each nation has a distinct "shape of refusal."

### Lithuania — the Catholic nation
- Pre-war **Kingdom under Vytautas II of Urach** — but the **King is tainted** (tried to deal with the Russians, fled to Sweden, declined the throne, retired to Normandy, died late 1950s; his son went into French banking). So the Lithuania-in-waiting is **republican**.

- Inside: the **Lietuvos Laisvės Taryba** (Freedom Council), partisan cells named for saints/heroes, the LKDP/LSDP/Liberal undergrounds.

- **Three resistance strands + the Pakt Kryvi:** (1) moderates (BNR/Hramada social-democrats); (2) **Green Oak / Zialiony Dub** (Łastoŭski's ultranationalists, the armed core in the Pinsk marshes); (3) the **orphaned left** (the Jezavitaŭ tradition, "the patron died with the Commune in 1943"). The one is a wound; the other is a heresy").

### Latvia & Estonia — the bound pair
- **The Forest Brothers (mežabrāļi)** — rebellion since the **1920s**, first anti-UBD, then anti-Russian ("Sixty years unbroken… You're born one"). **The United Baltic Duchy (UBD)** = the pre-war Baltic-German aristocracy (Knighthoods, Duke Adolf Friedrich), destroyed by Russia in 1944 — both peoples "fought the same barons before they fought the same Russians."

# XI. MOTIFS, REFRAINS & THE COQ-AND-AIGLE THEME

- **The Coq & the Aigle** (the deepest theme): the **Cockerel (Coq Gaulois)** that "crows and stands its ground on home soil" vs. the **Black Eagle (Reichsadler)** that "takes" — *"we are the bird that defends, they are the bird that seizes."* The Fourth Republic's deepest achievement is turning the two into one line of battle — "a Europe where the next war is fought **shoulder to shoulder instead of throat to throat**." Realized when **a fresh German army relieves Minsk (10 Aug '86)** — the nation that took Mathieu's leg in 1918 saves his great-grandson in 1986 ("the wound and the rescue in the same hand"). The ground refrain (the family told it "at the table for forty years"): **"Jamais dans l'histoire de France un Français n'a prié aussi fort pour que les Allemands arrivent."** Only Mathieu's 1914 generation can "hold both birds in the same hand."
- **The Pan Karol partisan tapestry:** Penthièvre, knocked out at the Drut and taken in by a Belarusian cell (recognized by his uniform — *"Frantsuz… The Frenchman"*); his **Saint-Cyr "soldier's Russian"** gives the cell "ears" (he reads radio nets and chalked rolling-stock signs, interrogates prisoners). **The Professor** (a Minsk historian driven to the marsh) recognizes the Bourbon name — *"La maison d'Orléans… Vous êtes de cette maison?" — "J'en suis. Mais ici je ne suis qu'un capitaine."* — and keeps the secret; their fireside friendship is "the only educated talk available." Deliberately **de-heroized: "one fighter in one cell among thousands… a grain in a landslide… he survived the winter; that was the whole of it. Not a hero."
- **Georges's captivity:** "not the camp — the gaze." Treated as **an exhibit** (guards bring friends to the wire to see "the *negr*"; a man with **calipers** measures his skull; he is "an argument" for visiting delegations). He **refuses to perform** — holds his face level and **runs load tables behind his eyes** ("the calipers measured a head that was somewhere else entirely"). The double-bind: **"To France he was too African to name his child. To Russia he was nothing but African."** Window in via the **Swedish Embassy (protecting power) + ICRC**; *disparu* → *prisonnier*; **the 25-word message** he may never receive, telling him he's a father.
- **Other refrains:** *"On est [N]. Il en manque [N]"* (the family arithmetic) · *"Le rien est le meilleur mot de la langue française"* (no news of your child in the reports) · *"La décision descend. Les noms remontent. Je vis dans les deux sens"* (Joseph) · *"certaines choses se disent debout… pas par lettre, en face"* (the women) · **hands** (Marc's tremor; Andy's unsteady hands holding André; Étienne stilling Marc's hands; Joseph's warm hands vs. the frozen front — *Martin-sans-gants*) · *"It's not nothing"* / damage as damage · *"Repose-toi"* (Joseph to Lucie, never obeyed — "the delivering was the love") · *"Dieu s'en fout / Dieu sait exactement ce qu'on fout là"* (Marc's worn faith) · the **locket** and the three Louis Henris · Gabriela's **blue nail-beds** and "the count" · the **censor** as quiet witness · *disparu*, "the useless word."
- **Deep themes:** reconciliation as the Republic's purpose; the wound and the rescue in the same nation; who can *understand* (the generational knowledge); grief delivered with victory ("the war does not deliver its joy and its sorrow on separate plates"); work as a wall against grief (Constance); the small and anonymous (Pan Karol; the *biffin* 2e classe "the least named"); the cost borne in silence (Joseph rising on a retreat that took his own blood); meritocracy vs. particles (Roquefeuil, the common Col. Moisson); the very old and the very new in one house.
